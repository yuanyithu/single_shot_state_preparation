# Exp102 q=0 direct-positive block full-column review

Version: `exp102.q0_random_full_column_direct_block.preflight.v1`

## Scientific question

Can the unchanged random-scan full-B-column heatbath be evaluated within the
frozen T1 resource cap by exploiting the certified positive dynamic range,
without changing its posterior, Markov clock, initialization panel, or
convergence gates?

The target remains

```text
H_Z e = y,
pi(e | y) proportional to (.04/.96)^|e|.
```

For one selected B column, candidate `x` has unnormalized collapsed weight

```text
(.04/.96)^|x| product_j M_p(a_j xor old xor x),
```

where the product runs over the fixed neighboring A factors.  Validation 053
evaluated the algebraically equivalent sum of logs, exponentiated all `2^24`
entries, and stored a 128 MiB CDF.  The successor multiplies the positive
factors directly, sums candidates in canonical order inside fixed `2^12`
blocks, stores only 4096 block subtotals, draws the same one portable uniform,
then recomputes only the selected block to locate the candidate.  It is an
exact coordinate heatbath in real arithmetic with a frozen deterministic
binary64 summation convention.

The optimization is permitted only when a state-independent conservative
lower bound on every candidate weight exceeds the smallest normal binary64 by
at least `2^20`.  No zero, subnormal, truncated, capped, renormalized, or
outcome-selected weight is allowed.  The planted error is not used in any
weight or transition.

## Blind spots addressed

- A fast update is irrelevant unless it still targets the hard-coset posterior;
  complete small-HGP transition matrices must pass detailed balance and
  stationarity, not only one conditional comparison.
- Ordinary state or logical-label changes can be generated by exact `A|B`
  redraws while B remains frozen.  Any later T1 must retain every B and logical
  diagnostic from validation 052.
- A common P/shifted-zero start can manufacture agreement.  The later T1 panel
  remains P, independent exact-K0 U, two B-distinct truth-free MAP families,
  and the eight B/logical-distinct low-energy S starts.  Physical zero is
  outside this nonzero-syndrome hard coset.
- Validation 053 showed that a macmini speedup does not predict Linux runtime.
  Local PASS can authorize only a fresh three-node preflight; only its
  fail-closed aggregate can authorize a separately frozen T1.
- The Linux legacy dense float path is not a portable byte oracle.  Correctness
  is instead established by complete small-code exact matrices, full m8
  algebraic weight comparisons with frozen numerical tolerances, and identical
  direct-block subtotal/transcript digests across nodes.

## Frozen correctness gates

1. On the `n=10,k=4` and `n=13,k=1` HGPs, zero/nonzero syndromes and
   `p=.04,.10,.25`, direct-block conditionals must match direct enumeration;
   complete random-scan transition matrices must have row-sum, detailed-balance,
   and stationarity errors at most `2e-13`.
2. Reference and Numba direct-block subtotal arrays and decisions must be
   byte-identical on the small oracles.  Raw trajectory replay must be bit
   exact.  Tampered cache, block partition, mass, hard-coset state, source,
   config, seed identity, or raw must be rejected.
3. For P/M0/S0/U0 and columns 0/11/17 on the frozen m8 sentinel, every one of
   the `2^24` direct positive weights is compared with the historical log
   formula after a common scale.  The maximum scaled-weight absolute error
   must be at most `5e-13`, maximum relative error at most `5e-12`, and total
   variation between normalized arrays at most `2e-12`.  All weights and all
   fixed block subtotals must be finite, positive, and normal.
4. The conservative log lower bound must exceed
   `log(float64.tiny)+log(2^20)` for every m8 probe.  The complete ordered block
   subtotal SHA catalog and the four portable sampling/replay transcript SHAs
   must agree on macmini, nd-1, nd-2, and nd-3.

## Frozen resource gates

- Numba is compiled in the parent before fork.  Four concurrent
  P/M0/S0/U0 probes each run 8 burn plus 128 measurement updates and complete
  replay; setup, sampling, observation, and replay are timed.
- The local median direct-block conditional must be at least `1.25x` faster
  than validation 053's streaming CDF on the same state/column.
- The worst projected replay-inclusive `2048+8192` trajectory, including an
  additional factor `1.2`, must be at most 7200 seconds on every node.
- No result-driven thread count, block size, attempt count, clock reduction,
  replay omission, or runtime extrapolation from a different state is allowed.

Any algebraic, underflow, direct/reference tolerance, subtotal digest, source,
or transcript failure is `CONFLICT`.  A local speed or any-node projection
failure is `RUNTIME_EXHAUSTED`.  Passing all local gates authorizes only a
fresh verified-source three-node preflight.  Passing its aggregate authorizes
only construction of a new T1 contract with fresh seeds/raw; it does not
authorize m6, HARD2, `q_top`, formal tuning, held-out, production, or a
physical conclusion.
