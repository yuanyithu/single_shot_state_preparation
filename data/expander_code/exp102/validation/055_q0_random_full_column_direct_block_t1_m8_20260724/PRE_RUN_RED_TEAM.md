# Validation 055 pre-run scientific red-team

This review is frozen before the final source schedule and any T1 measurement
raw.  Runtime convenience is subordinate to the questions below.

## Is the mathematical target still the deliverable target?

Yes.  The kernel samples the hard coset `H_Z e=y` with unnormalized weight
`(.04/.96)^|e|`.  The planted error is used only to construct P.  It is absent
from the direct weights, column clock, and `A|B` draw.  q_top is the posterior
logical-class purity estimated by the frozen character U-statistic; label
collision is not promoted to the primary estimator.

## Are all initial states legal and genuinely adversarial?

Yes.  The frozen control algebraically checks all 11 deterministic starts;
each U start is generated by an independent exact-K0 affine draw.  Physical
zero is illegal because this sentinel syndrome is nonzero, and shifted zero is
already P.  Replacing P/U/M0/M1/S with common P would hide failure to descend
from broad support or cross low-energy B basins.  The S same-B/different-label
control specifically tests whether visible logical motion is only `A|B`
conditional noise.

## Is the measured slow variable the one the kernel must transport?

The primary slow coordinate is collapsed B, not physical-state changes or
logical-label changes alone.  Raw records every B-column proposal and full B
trace.  Gates cover B bit/row/column/dense characters, B D2, B weight,
likelihood, constant-character burn crossing, and bidirectional M0/M1 visits.
Exact `A|B` redraws therefore cannot by themselves create a pass.

## Can all chains collapse into the same wrong basin and pass?

The combined defenses are P/U/MAP/S starts, family and pairwise q_top/D2,
full/B moments, constant-character crossing, and MAP reaction-coordinate
visits.  These do not constitute a theorem against every unobserved basin;
this remains a diagnostic, not formal certification.  A pass only authorizes
fresh m6 T1 and later independent HARD2 work.  It cannot remove the need for a
mechanistically independent confirmation method or formal held-out testing.

## Are the convergence thresholds tied to the actual need?

They are copied without relaxation from validation 052: q_top total SE .03,
absolute and SE-scaled family agreement, D2 upper .04, normalized full/B
weight and likelihood agreement, Rhat 1.05, nondegenerate bulk ESS 400, and
explicit transition/basin minima.  The test does not demand that every chain
visit every logical label; that would be stronger than posterior correctness
and could reject a legitimately concentrated posterior.  It demands agreement
on the delivery estimand and known slow-coordinate diagnostics.

## What implementation assumptions were not actually safe?

Three latent analyzer assumptions were found before launch:

1. Validation 052's analyzer never checked the direct kernel version or
   conditional-engine field, and its seed identity omitted the direct-version
   prefix.  Validation 055 binds all three exactly.
2. The old analyzer referenced `state_label` without importing it.  No 052 raw
   existed, so the fault had remained dormant.  Validation 055 imports and
   exercises it on a real miniature raw.
3. A generic sequential B-likelihood recomputation differs by one ULP from the
   kernel's fixed 1-D NumPy sum.  Validation 055 independently rebuilds every
   factor index but deliberately reproduces the frozen summation order; exact
   equality is then meaningful rather than a platform-sensitive false alarm.

The miniature test also tampers `conditional_engine` and requires fail-closed
rejection.  Full trajectory generation/replay remains bit-exact.

## What remains unproven even after a PASS?

A finite character panel and eight chains per family can share an unseen blind
spot; transition counts do not prove tail coverage; and this method cannot
independently confirm itself.  Therefore `DIAGNOSTIC_DIRECT_BLOCK_T1_M8_VIABLE`
is the maximum result.  It is not a q_top data point, `READY_FOR_FORMAL`, or a
license to run the 6144 production tasks.
